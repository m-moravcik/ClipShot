Undo now keeps up when you clean up several screenshots in a row.

- Every screenshot you move to the Trash, from the popover or from a floating preview, keeps its own **Moved to the Trash** strip with **Undo** in its own place in the list. A second move no longer takes away the Undo of the first.
- All of them stay until five seconds after your last move; then the rows below move up and older screenshots fill the list, as before. Nothing jumps while you are still deciding.
- **Undo** on a strip or a preview puts back that screenshot. ⌘Z puts back the last one you moved, and pressed again the one before.

Signed with Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Requires macOS 26.
